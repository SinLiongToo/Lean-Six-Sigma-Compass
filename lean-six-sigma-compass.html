    這個工具怎麼運作How this tool works

    1. 三項評分 + 截止日期 + 工時記錄1. Three scores + due date + hours logged

    每個任務有三項 1–5 分的評分——成本影響、品質影響、管理層偏好，一律「分數越高＝越優先」。此外可填寫截止日期，系統會據此計算「急迫度」。測試工時另外記錄，不列入加權分數。 Each task gets three 1–5 scores — Cost Impact, Quality Impact, Management Preference — higher is always higher priority. Optional due date calculates urgency. Testing hours logged separately.

    2. 正規化（Normalize）2. Normalization

    系統把三項評分換算成 0–100 的相對分數：最低值換為 0、最高值換為 100，其餘依比例內插。截止日則用固定公式：今天或已逾期 → 100 分；超過「急迫度視窗」天數（預設 30 天）→ 0 分；中間線性遞減。未設定截止日則為 0 分。 Converts 1–5 scores to a relative 0–100 scale: lowest becomes 0, highest becomes 100. Due date urgency uses a fixed formula: due today/overdue → 100; beyond the horizon (e.g. 30 days) → 0. No due date = 0.

    3. 權重百分比與優先分數3. Weights & Priority Score

    優先分數 = Σ（每項正規化分數 × 該項權重%），總合 0–100 分。67–100分為高優先（橘黃色），34–66分為中優先（綠色），0–33分為低優先。 Priority score = Σ (each normalized score × its weight%), yielding a 0–100 range. 67–100 is high (amber), 34–66 is medium (sage), 0–33 is low.

    精實會議指南Lean Meeting Guidelines

    高效的會議分為會前準備、會中互動、會後推進三個階段。本指南協助您終結「會而不議，議而不決」的浪費。 High-efficiency meetings are split into prep, interaction, and progress tracking. This guide eliminates the waste of decisionless meetings.

    🛠️ 會前準備：效率的基石Prep: The Foundation

    • 兩倍議程原則Double-Time Agenda Rule 會議前 24 小時必須發出議程。議程不只寫討論主題，還要明確寫出「每個項目的討論時間」與「預期要達到的結論」。 Agendas must be sent 24 hours in advance, explicitly detailing the time allocated per item and the target conclusion to be reached.
    • 極簡人數（雙披薩原則）Amazon's Two-Pizza Rule 只邀請「必須做決策」或「與執行直接相關」的人。人數越少，溝通成本越低，越容易推進。 Limit invitees to decision-makers and direct implementers. Fewer attendees means lower communication overhead and faster alignment.

    🗣️ 會中互動：高效與禮貌的平衡Interaction: Efficiency & Respect

    • 計時器制度 (Timeboxing)Timeboxing 每個議題時間一到就必須停止。若無法達成共識，直接指派專人課後研究，不允許單一議題無限超時。 Halt discussions immediately when the time limit expires. If no consensus is reached, delegate a DRI for offline research. Do not let single topics run over.
    • 對事不對人 (Issue-focused)Focus on the Issue 所有意見必須針對「提案內容」或「數據」，禁止使用「你每次都...」或「你的想法很扯」等人身、動機攻擊。 All feedback must target "proposals" or "data." Personal or motivational attacks like "you always..." or "that's ridiculous" are strictly banned.
    • 先傾聽再回應 (2分鐘不中斷)2-Min Uninterrupted Rule 當有人發言時，其他人不准插嘴。必須等發言者完整表達完（或限時 2 分鐘到），下一位才能舉手或指派發言。 No interruptions while someone is speaking. Wait for them to complete their point (or for the 2-minute mark) before raising your hand or speaking.
    • 建設性反對 (Yes, and...)Yes, and... (Constructive) 不能只說「這不可行」，禮貌且高效的做法是：「我理解這個方向，但考量到 A 風險，我建議可以調整為 B 方案。」 Never say "this won't work" without an alternative. Say: "I understand the direction, but considering Risk A, I suggest adjusting to Plan B."

    🚀 會後推進：終結「會而不議，議而不決」Progress: No Action, No Dismissal

    • 無行動不散會 (No Action, No Meeting)No Action, No Meeting 會議結束前最後 5 分鐘，主席必須口頭複誦並確認本次會議產出的所有「交辦事項（Action Items）」。 In the final 5 minutes, the chair must orally review and confirm all assigned Action Items. No meeting should end without actions.
    • 核心結論與摘要 (Key Takeaway & Summary)Key Takeaway & Summary 會議結束前應共同確認並宣讀「最重要的一點收穫 (Takeaway)」與「會議摘要 (Summary)」。將此結論記錄於備忘錄最上方，可確保全體與會者在離開時，對「核心決策與下一步共識」達成 100% 一致的認知，消除任何溝通偏差。 Before adjourning, define and confirm the single most critical "Key Takeaway" and "Summary." Recording this at the top of the memo ensures 100% alignment on decisions and prevents misaligned expectations after the meeting.
    • 3W 推進法則3W Progress Rule 每個交辦事項必須同時滿足以下三要素：
      - What：具體要做什麼（目標必須明確、可量化）。
      - Who：第一負責人（只能寫一個人，寫兩個等於沒人負責）。
      - When：截止日期（明確截止日期與時間）。       Every conclusion must specify:
      - What: Specific, quantifiable task.
      - Who: Single DRI (assigning two people means nobody is responsible).
      - When: A clear deadline.

    常見分析與認知謬誤Common Analytical & Cognitive Fallacies

    在進行流程改善與統計分析時，人性的心理偏誤與樂觀傾向常會扭曲客觀事實。了解這些常見謬誤，並利用系統化的防禦機制來對抗它們。 When performing process improvement and statistical analysis, human cognitive biases and optimistic tendencies often distort objective reality. Understand these common fallacies and use systemic defenses to counter them.

    ⚠️ 常見心理與計劃謬誤Common Cognitive & Planning Fallacies

    1. 規劃謬誤1. The Planning Fallacy

    高發Common

    表現形式：Manifestation: 傾向於低估專案所需的時間、成本與潛在風險，同時高估預期帶來的效益。Underestimating project duration, costs, and risks, while overestimating eventual benefits.

    LSS 現場示例：LSS Example: 綠帶團隊宣稱「只需 3 週就能全面重寫蝕刻機台的 SOP，並提升 5% 良率」，卻忽略了跨部門審查的溝通成本與機台排產衝突，最後耗時 3 個月才完成。A team plans to rewrite all etch tool SOPs and lift yield by 5% in 3 weeks, ignoring department alignment overhead and tool scheduling blocks, ending up taking 3 months.

    VUCA 時代視角：VUCA Perspective: 在易變且複雜的 VUCA（Volatile 易變、Uncertain 不確定、Complex 複雜、Ambiguous 模糊）時代，一味執著於消滅「計畫與實際（Plan vs. Actual）的落差」已不切實際。關鍵在於承認落差的存在，並透過快速試錯（Fail Fast）及調整 OKR（Objectives and Key Results，目標與關鍵成果）來因應變化。In the volatile VUCA (Volatile, Uncertain, Complex, Ambiguous) era, obsessing over eliminating the "plan vs. actual gap" is unrealistic. The key is to acknowledge the gap, learn by doing, and use agile trial-and-error (Fail Fast) alongside OKR (Objectives and Key Results) adjustments.

    2. 確認偏誤2. Confirmation Bias

    高發Common

    表現形式：Manifestation: 主觀上已經有了結論，因此只刻意尋找、採納支持該假說的數據，而忽視或貶低與假說矛盾的關鍵證據。Having a pre-conceived conclusion and selectively gathering data that supports it while discounting contradictory facts.

    LSS 現場示例：LSS Example: 工程師認定「黃光製程曝光能量過高是缺陷主因」，因此在分析數據時只拉出高曝光能量的批次，卻故意忽略低曝光能量批次同樣有高缺陷率的事實。An engineer suspects litho exposure energy causes defects, analyzing only high-exposure batches and ignoring that low-exposure runs exhibit identical defect levels.

    3. 沉沒成本謬誤3. Sunk Cost Fallacy

    高發Common

    表現形式：Manifestation: 因為先前已經投入了大量資金、時間或人力，明知該改進方向或專案無法成功，仍堅持繼續投入。Continuing investment in a failing strategy or LSS project simply because of the massive resources already poured into it.

    LSS 現場示例：LSS Example: 團隊花費了 5 萬美元與 4 個月時間對某舊型輸送帶進行自動化改造。進行到一半時，發現該機台已面臨淘汰，但因為「已經投入了那麼多時間金錢」，仍堅持硬著頭皮做完，造成更大的資源浪費。The team spent $50k and 4 months automating an old conveyor belt. Halfway through, they realize the belt is fundamentally obsolete, but they continue because "we already spent so much time and money on it."

    VUCA 與 OKR 應對：VUCA & OKR Alignment: 在 VUCA（Volatile 易變、Uncertain 不確定、Complex 複雜、Ambiguous 模糊）的環境下，為了推進市場必須「快快試錯並修正」。若里程碑落後，且舊指標已不符合現狀，應依據 OKR（Objectives and Key Results，目標與關鍵成果）指標動態調整或終止專案，絕不能因沉沒成本而拒絕改變。Faced with VUCA (Volatile, Uncertain, Complex, Ambiguous) dynamics, trying and failing fast is key to market progression. If milestone metrics fall behind and cease to reflect reality, adjust or terminate the project according to OKR (Objectives and Key Results) indicators, never resisting pivots due to sunk costs.

    4. 倖存者偏差4. Survivorship Bias

    高發Common

    表現形式：Manifestation: 只關注篩選過程後「倖存下來」的數據或對象，卻忽視了那些在篩選過程中被淘汰的更關鍵樣本。Focusing exclusively on data or items that survived a selection process while ignoring those that failed and dropped out.

    LSS 現場示例：LSS Example: 分析機台運轉參數時，只收集「順利生產完成的晶圓」數據，卻完全排除「在製程中破裂、中途報廢的晶圓」參數，導致算出的 Cp/Cpk 異常完美，隱蔽了真實問題。Calculating process capability using only wafers that successfully completed production, while discarding broken/scrapped runs, yielding deceptively high Cpk values.

    5. 後因謬誤 (以先後為因果)5. Post Hoc Fallacy

    高發Common

    表現形式：Manifestation: 僅因為事件 B 發生在事件 A 之後，就認定事件 A 是事件 B 的原因。忽略了這可能只是時間上的巧合，或存在其他真正的主因。Concluding that Event A caused Event B simply because Event B occurred after Event A, ignoring coincidence or other hidden variables.

    LSS 現場示例：LSS Example: 機台更換了皮帶後，隔天產能異常提升。團隊便對外宣稱「更換皮帶成功解決了產能瓶頸」，卻忽略了同一天剛好有大批熟練的資深人員換班上線，這才是真實主因。After changing a conveyor belt, throughput rises the next day. The team credits the belt change, ignoring that a highly experienced shift crew took over the line at the same time, which was the actual cause of the lift.

    對應修正機制：Correction: 應使用 實戰工具 7：MSA 量測系統分析 排除儀器飄移或操作員偏誤；並以 實戰工具 8：實驗設計 (DOE) 或 實戰工具 6：假設檢定 進行雙樣本對照，驗證統計上的因果顯著性。Use Tool 7: MSA to rule out gauge drift/operator bias; use Tool 8: DOE or Tool 6: Hypothesis Test to confirm causal significance.

    6. 因果謬誤 (混淆相關與因果)6. Correlation vs. Causation Fallacy

    高發Common

    表現形式：Manifestation: 當兩組數據在統計上呈現高度相關性（Correlation）時，便草率認定兩者之間具有因果關係，忽視了「第三共變因」或「因果關係倒置」的可能性。Assuming a causal relationship exists between two variables simply because they are statistically correlated, overlooking confounding variables or reverse causality.

    LSS 現場示例：LSS Example: 統計顯示「產線上巡檢員的走動步數」與「報廢晶圓數量」呈高度正相關。主管因此得出錯誤結論：「巡檢員走動過多導致了晶圓報廢，應限制其走動」，實則是晶圓報廢量增多時，巡檢員必須頻繁走動處理異常。A regression shows inspectors' daily step count is highly correlated with wafer scrap counts. Leadership falsely concludes that excessive walking causes scrap and restricts movement, failing to see that high scrap rates force inspectors to walk more to address issues.

    對應修正機制：Correction: 應藉由 實戰工具 8：實驗設計 (DOE) 操縱變數進行主動實驗以確定因果鏈；或使用 實戰工具 9：迴歸分析 控制干擾因子，並以 實戰工具 10：ANOVA 檢定不同因子水準的均值差異。Execute active experimentation using Tool 8: DOE to determine the causal chain, or apply Tool 9: Regression and Tool 10: ANOVA to control and test variables.

    7. 可得性啟發法 (易得性偏差)7. Availability Heuristic

    高發Common

    表現形式：Manifestation: 傾向於根據大腦中最容易想起、最新鮮或最震撼的記憶與事件，來評估某個事件發生的頻率或重要性，而非依據客觀的統計數據與機率分布。Overestimating the likelihood or severity of events based on how easily examples come to mind — typically relying on recent, dramatic, or memorable events rather than objective statistical probabilities.

    LSS 現場示例：LSS Example: 產線昨天剛發生一起「氣壓閥失效導致晶圓碎裂」的嚴重事件。今天分析品質異常時，團隊便直覺認定「氣壓問題是目前良率低迷的主因」，並花費大量資源拆修所有氣壓系統，卻忽視了長期的品質柏拉圖（Pareto）數據顯示氣壓失效佔比不到 1%，真正的瓶頸是化學液濃度漂移。After a dramatic "pneumatic valve failure causing wafer breakage" event yesterday, the team immediately asserts "pneumatic pressure is the main driver of our yield loss," wasting resources inspecting valve lines while ignoring historical Pareto data showing pneumatic failure accounts for < 1%.

    對應修正機制：Correction: 應強迫團隊回歸數據，利用 實戰工具 2：VSM 價值流程圖 或品質歷史柏拉圖 (Pareto Chart) 進行長期定量統計，排除因單一顯著事件造成的認知偏差，以數據客觀排列改善優先權。Force the team to return to data, utilizing long-term historical Pareto Charts or Tool 2: VSM quantitative analysis to bypass cognitive bias and prioritize actions objectively.

    防禦原理：Principle: 藉由第三方或黑帶（Black Belt）專家對數據、MSA、假設檢定前提進行審查。當事人往往身處局中，無法看見盲點，同儕審查能以客觀視角挑戰假設，排除確認偏誤。Independent BBs or MBBs review datasets, Gage R&R, and test assumptions. Since creators have blind spots, peers act as an objective filter to challenge hypotheses.

    精實實務：Best Practice: 要求所有六標準差專案在進入 Improve（改善）階段前，必須由非專案組的黑帶進行 Tollgate Review（階段門審查），挑戰其 Measure 階段數據的信度。Require all project teams to pass a Tollgate Review with independent Black Belts before transitioning from Analyze to Improve, validating data integrity.

    標準化與 SOP (Standardization)SOP & Standardization

    機制Defense

    防禦原理：Principle: 人性的另一個弱點是「惰性」與「投機取巧」（如《自律就是自由》所述）。標準化是去個人化的機制，它將最佳實踐固化為作業規範，利用剛性約束對抗個人隨意的「心理調整」或「經驗主義」。Standardization depersonalizes actions. Codifying best practices into rigid workflows acts as a barrier against emotional, ad-hoc adjustments or shortcuts by operators.

    精實實務：Best Practice: 一旦發現製程改善配方，立即將參數設定寫入機台 Recipe 機制與點檢 SOP 中，配合防呆（Poka-Yoke）互鎖，使得任何人想偷懶或取巧都無法啟動機台。Lock parameters directly into machine recipes and checklists, using physical or software Poka-Yoke to block manual bypasses or laziness.

    歷史案例對照 (Historical Anchoring)Historical Benchmarks

    機制Defense

    防禦原理：Principle: 對抗「規劃謬誤」的最佳武器是「參考類別預測（Reference Class Forecasting）」。估計專案時程與效益時，不應依賴主觀推導，而應查閱「歷史上類似專案的實際數據」作為基準點。Counter the planning fallacy using Reference Class Forecasting. Rather than relying on optimistic projections, pull actual data from similar past projects.

    精實實務：Best Practice: 建立「精實專案資料庫」（如本系統的案例筆記與效益計算歷史）。申報新專案時，審查委員需比對過往同類型設備改善專案的平均時程與回收期，若偏差過大需提出科學化證明。Maintain an LSS project database. When reviewing a charter, benchmark proposed savings and schedules against similar historical equipment modifications.

    敏捷試錯與 OKR 里程碑 (Agile & OKR Milestones)Agile & OKR Milestones

    機制Defense

    防禦原理：Principle: 在易變且模糊的 VUCA 時代，計畫（Plan）與實際（Actual）存在落差是必然常態。與其過度執著於消滅落差，不如承認其存在，以「做中學、學中做、快快 Fail、快快修正」為核心。透過設立明確的里程碑與 OKR，動態檢視並隨時修正航道，而非盲目死守最初的計畫。In a VUCA world, gaps between plan and actual are inevitable. Instead of obsessing over eliminating discrepancies, embrace "learning by doing, failing fast, and correcting fast". Establish clear milestones to evaluate if OKRs are on track or need adjusting.

    精實實務：Best Practice: 將長週期計畫拆解為週或雙週的「實踐風暴（Try-Storming）」循環。每一階段聚焦在驗證關鍵假設，並在里程碑節點檢視 OKR 達成度。若發現方向偏離或假設有誤，立即在下一個 PDCA 循環中進行調整修正。Break long-term projects into weekly or bi-weekly "Try-Storming" iterations. Focus on validating critical assumptions at each milestone and assess OKR health. If assumptions prove wrong, pivot immediately in the next PDCA cycle.

    • 降低品質不良成本 (COPQ)：半導體製程極其複雜，良率 (Yield) 的 1% 波動常代表數百萬美元的損益。透過 SPC 與 DMAIC 專案控制製程漂移，直接降低報廢與返工成本，能立即拉升毛利率。 COPQ Reduction: Semiconductor steps are highly sensitive. A 1% yield shift equals millions in P&L. Controlling process drift via SPC and DMAIC directly cuts scrap costs, raising gross margin.
    • 提高設備綜合效率 (OEE)：高精密設備（如 Scanner / Implanter）折舊高昂。運用 TPM 及 SMED (快速換線) 減少非預期停機，能有效分攤固定製造費用 (Overheads)。 OEE Improvement: Highly precise machinery carries massive depreciation. Running TPM and SMED to compress idle time spreads overheads across higher output volumes.
