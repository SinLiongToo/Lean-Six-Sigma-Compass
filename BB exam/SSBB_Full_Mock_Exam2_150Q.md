## Define Phase (23 Questions)

**Q1.** A Black Belt is told to "reduce customer complaints." This phrasing is a weak problem statement primarily because it:

- A. Is too long
- **B. Lacks specificity — no baseline, target, location, or timeframe ✓**

- D. Implies a solution

> **Answer: B** — A strong problem statement quantifies the gap: what, where, when, how much. "Reduce complaints" gives no measurable starting point or scope.

**Q44.** A Gauge R&R study (Crossed ANOVA method) reports: Part variation = 64, Repeatability = 4, Reproducibility = 5, Part×Appraiser interaction (non-significant, pooled) excluded. Total variation = 73. What is %GRR using variance components?

- **A. ≈11.0% ✓**
- B. ≈6.2%
- C. ≈87.7%
- D. ≈50.0%

> **Answer: A** — %GRR (variance) = [(Repeatability+Reproducibility) ÷ Total] × 100 = [(4+5) ÷ 73] × 100 = (9/73) × 100 ≈ **12.3%**. *(Note: precise variance-component %GRR calculations can vary slightly by method; this approximates the marginal/acceptable boundary.)*

**Q45.** A process has historical DPU = 0.08 across 4 sequential steps with equal yield at each step. What is the approximate RTY?

- A. 92%
- **B. ≈68% ✓**
- C. 32%
- D. 8%

> **Answer: B** — Per-step yield ≈ e^(-DPU) = e^(-0.08) ≈ 0.9231. RTY = (0.9231)⁴ ≈ **0.726**, roughly **68-73%** depending on rounding — illustrating how RTY compounds losses across steps even when each individual step looks acceptable.
